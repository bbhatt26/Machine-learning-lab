SALES ANALYSIS - KEY FINDINGS
==============================
1. Total sales Rs 156,474,598, total profit Rs 17,057,858 (overall margin 10.9%).
2. Best month: November 2023 with sales of Rs 6,319,175. Sales peak in Oct-Dec (festive season).
3. Technology generates the most sales; Technology generates the most profit.
4. Top product by sales: Laptop Pro (Rs 54,070,500).
5. Region with highest sales: North; lowest profit margin: North (10.5%).
6. 14.1% of orders are loss-making; losses are concentrated in orders with 20-30% discount.
7. RFM segmentation: 200 'Champion' customers contribute Rs 68,079,492 revenue.
Recommendations: cap discounts at 10%, stock up before Oct, run retention offers for 'At Risk' customers,
and focus on the high-margin Office Supplies category for profit growth.
